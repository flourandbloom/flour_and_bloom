Recipes from the Kitchen

Three bakes we have on repeat. Tested in a home oven, until they work every time.

Read the whole recipe before you start. The trick to baking is reading twice and mixing once. All weights are in grams — please do not use cups for these. A kitchen scale is the difference between a flat cake and a tall one.

Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies

Makes 12 cookies · 45 min + 1 hour chill

Ingredients

  • 225 g unsalted butter
  • 200 g light brown sugar
  • 80 g white sugar
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 310 g all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda + 1 tsp fine sea salt
  • 250 g dark chocolate, chopped into chunks
  • Flaky sea salt, for topping

Method

  1. Brown the butter over medium heat, swirling, until nutty and amber — about 6 minutes. Cool until barely warm.
  2. Whisk the cooled butter with both sugars until glossy. Add eggs and vanilla, whisk until smooth.
  3. Stir in flour, baking soda, and salt. Fold in the chocolate.
  4. Chill the dough for at least 1 hour. Overnight is better.
  5. Bake at 180°C for 12–14 minutes until edges are set but centres still look soft. Tap the tray twice on the counter.
  6. Sprinkle with flaky salt. Cool on the tray for 10 minutes.

Beginner's Sourdough Loaf

Makes 1 loaf · 24 hours, mostly hands-off

You will need an active, bubbly sourdough starter.

Ingredients

  • 500 g bread flour
  • 350 g warm water
  • 100 g active starter
  • 10 g fine sea salt

Method

  1. Mix flour and water until no dry bits remain. Rest 1 hour (autolyse).
  2. Add starter and salt. Pinch and squeeze together until combined.
  3. Bulk ferment 4–6 hours at room temp. Stretch and fold every 30 min for the first 2 hours.
  4. When dough is jiggly and grown by 50%, shape into a tight round.
  5. Place seam-up in a floured banneton. Refrigerate overnight.
  6. Heat oven to 250°C with Dutch oven inside for 45 minutes.
  7. Score the cold dough, lower into the hot pot, cover.
  8. Bake covered 20 min, uncovered 20–25 min until deep brown.
  9. Cool completely on a rack — at least 1 hour — before slicing.

Vanilla Bean Layer Cake

One 6-inch, 3-layer cake · 2 hours + cooling

For the cake

  • 225 g unsalted butter, softened
  • 300 g caster sugar
  • 4 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1 vanilla bean, seeds scraped
  • 280 g cake flour + 2 tsp baking powder + ½ tsp salt
  • 180 ml whole milk, room temperature

For the buttercream

  • 340 g unsalted butter, softened
  • 450 g icing sugar, sifted
  • 1 vanilla bean, seeds scraped
  • 2–3 tbsp double cream

Method

  1. Heat oven to 170°C. Grease and line three 6-inch round tins.
  2. Cream butter and sugar for 4 full minutes — pale and fluffy.
  3. Add eggs one at a time, then vanilla.
  4. Add dry mix in three additions, alternating with milk. Start and end with flour.
  5. Divide between tins, bake 25–28 min until a skewer comes out clean.
  6. For buttercream: beat butter alone for 5 minutes. Add icing sugar in two additions, then vanilla and cream. Beat 3 more minutes.
  7. Level cakes if domed. Stack with buttercream. Crumb-coat, chill, then final coat.
