Welcome to Flour & Bloom

A small home bakery built on three things — good flour, real butter, and patience.

Every loaf is naturally leavened over twenty-four hours. Every cake is layered by hand. We bake in small batches so nothing leaves the kitchen until it is right — the crust crackling, the crumb open, the buttercream silky.

Whether you came here for a recipe to try at home, a gallery to scroll through, or to order something special, we are glad you stopped by. Pull up a chair, the kettle is on.

What We Bake

Our most-loved bakes, available by order.

Country Sourdough

A 24-hour fermented loaf with a deep mahogany crust and an open, custardy crumb. Naturally leavened, no commercial yeast.

Brown Butter Cookies

Sea-salted, chewy in the centre, crisp at the edges. We brown the butter for an extra layer of toffee flavour.

Vanilla Bean Layer Cake

Three layers of buttery vanilla sponge with Madagascar vanilla buttercream. Made to order for birthdays.
